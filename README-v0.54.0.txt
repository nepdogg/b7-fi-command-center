FINAL 9-CENTER NAVIGATION
Operations Center | Tool Center | Shipping Center | Priority Center | Status Center |
Meeting Center | Action Center | Reference Center | Administration Center

CONSOLIDATIONS
- Tool Center: current quarter tools + Tool Archive only. All Active Tools removed.
- Priority Center: Weekday + Weekend.
- Status Center: Morning Status + Leads Extra Status.
- Action Center is the destination for tasks/assignments/status-bar actions; Lead Center removed from navigation.
- Reference Center: FI Knowledge + Reference Files.
- Administration Center: Admin Home + Data & Backup + Wallboard Configuration.
- Customer Requirements remains tool-level workflow rather than top-level navigation.
- Wallboard is launched from Operations Center and configured in Administration Center.

- One Action Center entry.
